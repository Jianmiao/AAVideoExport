The experimental Direct3D11 backend bundles Vortice.Direct3D11, DXGI,
DirectX and D3DCompiler 2.4.2, Vortice.Mathematics 1.4.25, and
SharpGen.Runtime / Runtime.COM 2.0.0-beta.13 (.NET 6 assemblies).
Their MIT licenses and copyright notices are included in docs/third-party.
The HLSL backend derives the CNN and RCAS equations from the embedded shaders;
the original bloc97 and AMD license headers remain in those resources.

The clone-ready version directory contains mod/core assemblies, the seven
graphics dependency assemblies, settings manifest and an authored icon.
The repository also includes source, tests and documentation. FFmpeg must be separately
obtained under its distribution's license. AA/game/character/audio resources
remain local to their owner. No Jianying code, icon, image or model is copied.

`src/VideoExport.Plugin/Research/Il2CppBulkCopy.cs` originates from the user's
Unity native-export research package dated 2026-10-01; the bulk-copy algorithm
is integrated at the user's request. It is not AA decompiled implementation code.
No independent license declaration was supplied for that research file.
It is retained with its provenance in this maintainer-requested publication;
the repository's MIT license does not relicense it or grant additional rights
to that contribution. Its separate licensing terms remain unspecified.

The 2026-10-03 performance integration branch adds user-side research-derived
proxy capture sizing, a fused resize/Rec.709 pass, owned native frame buffers and
native pipe submission. The original collaborator retains authorship of the
original UI foundation, story loading, native layout/audio and export lifecycle.
Subsequent user-side work redesigns the panel and adds super-resolution,
Direct3D/NV12 integration, device policy, Mbps controls and localized errors;
retained original implementation is not reattributed to the later contributor.
Historical line-count comparisons are not included as author-history evidence.
This publication preserves the research-origin boundary and does not bundle
compiler/runtime tools.

The super-resolution update embeds Anime4K v3.2 Upscale Denoise CNN x2 L
(Copyright 2019–2021 bloc97, MIT) and the agyild mpv LUMA-plane port of
AMD FSR1 v1.0.2 (Copyright 2021 Advanced Micro Devices, Inc., MIT).
Embedded shaders preserve their full license headers. The Anime4K derivative
changes only the ten activation thresholds from >1.2x to >1.0x, retaining
the network weights. The RGB RCAS GLSL adaptation follows AMD's FSR1
equations, preserves AMD's license and uses a guarded division for constant
black/white regions. Parameter strength 0..1 follows the supplied Magpie UI.
See docs/shader-provenance-2026-10-03.md for upstream URLs and hashes.
FFmpeg/libplacebo and Vulkan drivers are external host dependencies, not
redistributed with this mod. No XeSS or DLSS runtime is bundled.
